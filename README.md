# Amazon Financial Analysis & Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon.com's financial performance using Microsoft Excel. It combines historical financial statements, financial ratio analysis, business segment analysis, financial forecasting, and discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation into a consolidated financial analysis project.

## Objectives

- Evaluate revenue growth and profitability trends.
- Analyze the performance of Amazon's major business segments.
- Assess liquidity, leverage, and cash flow generation.
- Develop financial forecasts for FY2026–FY2028.
- Estimate intrinsic value using a DCF valuation model.
- Present key financial insights through an Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Financial statement analysis
- Financial ratio analysis
- Data visualization
- Financial forecasting
- Discounted cash flow valuation

## Project Components

1. **Income Statement Analysis** — Revenue, expenses, operating income, net income, and profitability margins.
2. **Balance Sheet Analysis** — Assets, liabilities, shareholders' equity, liquidity, and leverage.
3. **Cash Flow Analysis** — Operating cash flow, capital expenditure, and free cash flow.
4. **Segment Analysis** — Revenue trends across online stores, physical stores, third-party seller services, advertising, subscriptions, AWS, and other activities.
5. **Financial Modelling** — Revenue, profitability, and cash flow projections for FY2026–FY2028.
6. **Valuation** — DCF model based on forecast cash flows and explicit valuation assumptions.
7. **Interactive Dashboard** — Visual summary of financial performance and key metrics.

## Key Findings

- Revenue increased from approximately $574.8 billion in FY2023 to $716.9 billion in FY2025.
- Operating margin improved from approximately 6.4% in FY2023 to 11.2% in FY2025.
- Advertising services and AWS were among the fastest-growing major business segments in FY2025.
- Higher capital expenditure significantly affected free cash flow in FY2025, despite stronger operating cash flow.
- The analysis highlights the importance of evaluating profitability, cash generation, capital allocation, and growth together.

## Methodology & Limitations

Historical financial figures are analyzed using the data contained in the workbook. Forecasts and valuation outputs depend on assumptions about future performance, discount rates, and terminal growth.

The DCF valuation is an illustrative model for educational purposes and should not be interpreted as investment advice or a definitive estimate of market value.
